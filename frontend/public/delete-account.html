Delete your Atomize account and data

To request deletion of your Atomize account and associated data, email contact@atomize.online with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Include the email address used to sign in so we can verify your request.

We will remove your account profile, tasks, subtasks, shared-task records, and friend connections from active systems. This process is typically completed within 30 days.
